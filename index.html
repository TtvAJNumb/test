About Us

Tequila Indianos is more than a drink – it's a celebration of Mexico's vibrant spirit. Born from four generations of ranching and farming, our mission is to craft the world's finest tequila sustainably and with soul. Each bottle carries the flavors, aromas, and emotions that reflect the land, history, and extraordinary people behind it.

Our Story

Our name honors the Indianos – Spanish pioneers who crossed oceans with only dreams and grit. Their legacy brought ranching, farming, and cowboy traditions to the Americas. Today, Tequila Indianos embodies that same pioneering spirit, blending tradition, craftsmanship, and culture in every bottle.

TEQUILA INDIANS

Imagine traveling to a new place with only hope in your heart and the determination to succeed. That is the story of an extraordinary group of individuals, the Indians.

Those people who, whether motivated by adventure or by the dream of gaining access to a better standard of living, left their places of origin are called Indians. His achievements and legacy testify to his courageous attitude, his entrepreneurial spirit and altruistic heart.

Through Indianos we meet the Spanish emigrant who went out in search of a better life in the Americas, and who returns after having achieved his dreams of wealth and prosperity. These migratory flows were experienced between the 16th and early 20th centuries, to countries such as Cuba, Uruguay, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Venezuela, Argentina and Mexico. They were called Indianos because when America was discovered, the Spanish thought they arrived in the Indies and called the natives Indians, and the Spaniards and their descendants who lived there Indians.

The Indians were the ones who introduced many of the ancient traditions and customs to America, such as architecture, technology, cuisine, music and culture, they brought horses, bulls, roosters, livestock and tools to work the fields, so their contributions helped boost the economy of different regions; it can be said that it was the Indians who influenced the beginning of the carnivals that are celebrated in many cities and ports in Latin America, and who contributed country customs to give birth to the Charrería that was practiced in the plains at the beginning of the 20th century, and which today is the national sport par excellence, and its emblematic clothing is one of the most representative of Mexico.

Selection and Jima

Our Agave Azul fields are located in a privileged land, in the heart of the state of Jalisco, a volcanic area rich in minerals, with a climate of temperate temperature, lulled by the wind and the ode of birds, where the abundant sun and pristine rainwater nourishes plants that take up to eight years to mature, creating a perfect gift from nature.

Fermentation and Distillation

Master Tequileros are artists by nature, gifted with the gift of creating emotions, which is transmitted from generation to generation, transforming the treasure of nature into an exceptional collection of authentic works of art.

Striving to produce the best tequila, our artisanal production process begins the old-fashioned way, with the slow cooking of the agave plants to maintain their intense aromas, rich in smoky flavor and natural sweetness. Our legendary fermentation and distillation process is carried out in small batches, under the constant supervision of our Master Tequilero, always applying strict quality control throughout the process, using artisanal methods and new technologies in order to achieve optimal results. The result is a Tequila with a perfect balance between softness, rich flavor and subtle aromas.

Aging and Bottling

Indian Tequila it is aged in white oak barrels to maintain its exquisite aromas, give it maturity and refined character. Maestro Tequilero inspects and verifies that each batch meets our standards and strict quality controls to be bottled at the place of origin, and satisfy the most demanding palates in the world.

It is an authentic product, made in Mexico, 100% Blue Agave variety Tequilana Weber, original packaging under supervision and with authorization from the Tequila Regulatory Council.

OUR BLENDS

Each Tequila Indianos expression is crafted with its own distinct character, aging process, and flavor profile — blending generations of craftsmanship with the finest Blue Agave to create a truly unforgettable tequila experience.

“Made with passion in Jalisco to be shared with the world”

The collection of Indian Tequila it should not be missing from the cellar of good hosts who like to share products of extraordinary quality. Above and beyond the extraordinary quality of our collection, these creations celebrate an artistic vision of the peasant to satisfy personal tastes, and speak to stories inspired by exceptional personalities from around the world.

Our collection is the forge and interpretation of peasant love and centuries of history in perfect harmony with nature, it represents the essence and spirit of the Mexican artisan, a symbol of ancestry in which the past, present and future converge, creating an extraordinary tequila to be shared around the table with feelings of friendship, love and brotherhood.
